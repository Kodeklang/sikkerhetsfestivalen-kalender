Cyber Crime

Hunting international cyber criminals

Onsdag 10:00–10:40 9 · Victoria Scandic, sal 1+2

NorwegianHigh-level 40 min

Foredragsholder

Håvard Aalmo

Head of High-Tech Crime Section, National Cybercrime Centre (NC3), KRIPOS, Norway

Aalmo is head of the High-Tech Crime Section at NC3/Kripos. He has more than 25 years of experience from criminal investigations, spanning from violent crimes, international criminal cases (war crimes) and cybercrime. As the manager of an investigation section at Kripos he also participates closely in the international police cooperation through Europol and Interpol.

Om sesjonen

This session will give insight in the role of the police in the prevention of cyber crime, and how information gathering, international cooperation and use of different methods enables the police to investigate and prosecute highly sophisticated criminals.
