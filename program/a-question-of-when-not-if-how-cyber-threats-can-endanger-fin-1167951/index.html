Om sesjonen

The transition from localized ICT incidents to systemic financial shocks is no longer a theoretical risk—it is a macro-prudential reality. As the European financial sector navigates the post-DORA landscape, understanding the transmission channels of cyber contagion is critical for both regulators and industry leaders.

This session deconstructs how operational disruptions escalate into systemic crises. We will move beyond individual institutional resilience to examine the "plumbing" of the European financial architecture, focusing on the concentration risks of critical service providers and the role of the EU-SCICF in coordinated crisis response. Join this session for a rigorous look at how we move from preventing the "if" to absorbing the "when."
