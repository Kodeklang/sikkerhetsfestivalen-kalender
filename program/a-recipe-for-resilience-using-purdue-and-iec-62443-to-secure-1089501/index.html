A Recipe for Resilience: Using Purdue and IEC 62443 to Secure Europe's Food Supply

Tirsdag 10:00–10:40 2 · Home hotel Hammer, moen nede

EnglishHigh-level 40 min

Foredragsholdere

Henrik Jenssen

Head of Information Security, Orkla Foods

Henrik is Head of Information Security at Orkla Foods, a major European food manufacturer and distributor. With extensive experience in information security governance, risk management, and business continuity, he focuses on building resilience across the entire value chain, highlighting the enabling value of information security.

Tony Iskandar

Orkla Foods Norway, Head of digital

Tony is an experienced digital transformation leader with a global background across the Middle East, North Africa, and Europe. With a career spanning oil & gas and food manufacturing, he has led large-scale digitalization initiatives focused on automation, data-driven decision-making, and operational efficiency. Having lived and worked in multiple countries, Tony brings a broad international perspective and a strong ability to navigate complex organizational environments. Today, he oversees digitalization for food factories in Norway, driving security , efficient production and innovation through IoT, integrated systems and AI.

Om sesjonen

This presentation will showcase how Orkla Foods, a company of more than 5000 employees and 35 factories in 10+ countries, is working to use the Purdue Model and IEC 62443 to secure factories, in order to enable safe digitalisation and AI adoption. We will share our vision for future-ready factories, the business case for robust OT security, and some of the practical steps we have taken to pursue the Purdue Model and IEC 62443 standards.
