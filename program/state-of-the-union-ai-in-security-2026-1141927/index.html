Om sesjonen

Frontier AI agents escaping their test environments and hacking other companies, autonomous cyberattacks targeting governments and corporations, frontier vs. open source — what a time to be alive in security!

Join us for a lively discussion on the state of AI in cybersecurity so far in 2026. We’ll touch on the latest developments in AI, how harnesses are being built around increasingly capable models, the status of the vulnocolypse, and what we’re observing from conversations with customers.

We’ll also look ahead at what all of this means for the cybersecurity industry itself: how security vendors may need to evolve, what tomorrow’s products could look like, and whether the way we buy and use security technology is about to fundamentally change.
