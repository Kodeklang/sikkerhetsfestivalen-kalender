Application Security

Inside the NPM supply chain attacks: Lessons from a worm on the loose

Tirsdag 15:00–15:40 1 · Frimurerlosjen, rom 1

EnglishDetailed 40 min

Foredragsholder

Nora Tomas

Senior Security Engineer

Nora is an Application Security Engineer at Storebrand, where she focuses on helping developers build secure systems and defend against threats.

Before moving into security, Nora spent several years as a developer specializing in authentication solutions, giving her an understanding of the challenges developers face.

Today, she leverages that experience to bridge the gap between development and security, working on strategies, tools, and practices that strengthen application security across the organization.

Om sesjonen

At the end of last year, the NPM ecosystem was hit with three supply‑chain attacks in rapid succession. In this session, we will go through the technical details of each one, how they unfolded, and how they impacted Storebrand.

From a crypto interceptor to Shai‑Hulud 1.0 and 2.0, you will learn how our AppSec team worked closely with developers and detection engineering to respond quickly and create mitigations by developing new scanners, as well as by leveraging our existing tool‑stack.

Further, we will look at long‑term strategies that can help developers protect against these types of attacks. We will also explain how to scale these strategies across a large organization such as Storebrand, with 300+ developers, 17 GitHub organizations, and varied tool‑stacks.

By the end of the session, you will leave with practical ideas for short‑term defenses you can use during the next NPM supply‑chain attack, and long‑term strategies to strengthen your stack.
