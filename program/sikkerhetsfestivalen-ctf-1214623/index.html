Om sesjonen

Join us in Festsalenen at Kulturhuset for a beginner-friendly team based Capture The Flag (CTF) competition.

Whether you're new to cybersecurity or looking to hone your skills, this CTF offers challenges for all skill levels. Participants will tackle a series of challenges designed to test your problem-solving abilities and introduce you to various aspects of cybersecurity like cryptography, reverse engineering and web security.
