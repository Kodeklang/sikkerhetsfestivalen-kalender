Om sesjonen

The world is changing and so are we, but are we really?

In todays political climate, where there hybrid warfare is a reality, with enemies who does not care which side of the SCADA system you are on, the time has come to tackle the never ending conflict: OT vs. IT.

In this session, Amalie Lønning is inviting both IT and OT security professionals in a panel debate, addressing the challenges lying ahead to secure our most critical functions of society, breaking down silos and bridging the gap between IT and OT.
